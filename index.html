Q&A

Who are you?

My name is Anvy— your neighbor in 5D! I’ve lived in the building for almost six years with my husband, Mark, and our dog, Maxx. Most recently, we welcomed our baby, Avyn, to the family!

Why are you doing this?

I’m on maternity leave and developed a love for hand whisking lattes with high-quality ingredients. I enjoy making them exactly how I like them, especially while being mindful of caffeine during breastfeeding. Now I want to turn this little morning ritual into a mini side quest—something creative that’s just for me outside of full-time stay-at-home mom life.

What are the drinks sweetened with?

All drinks are sweetened with maple syrup.

Where are the ingredients from?

The matcha and hojicha are organic and from a farm in the countryside of Kyoto, Japan, which I personally visited and took a class at last year! All other ingredients are from Whole Foods, and the ice is from Target.
